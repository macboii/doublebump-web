DoubleBump ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Data We Collect

Account & Profile

  • Name and email address — provided by Apple or Google at sign-in, or entered by you if you sign up with email
  • Nickname — chosen by you during setup
  • Profile photo — optionally uploaded by you
  • Social media handles — optionally added by you (Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Discord, GitHub, Spotify, Pinterest, Reddit, YouTube, Email)

Digital Profile Card (optional)

Your profile card holds the details you choose to exchange with people you meet. Every field is optional and entered by you:

  • Display name, headline/job title, company, city (typed by you — not taken from GPS), short bio, and website
  • Phone number
  • Contact email — a separate field from the email you sign in with. If we copy your sign-in email into this field for you, it stays private until you confirm who can see it
  • Card handle — the short name used in your web card address (doublebump.com/card/your-handle)

You choose who can see each contact field:

  • Public — anyone who can view your card, including your web card if you turn it on
  • Connections — only people you have met and bumped with
  • Private — only you

Your phone number is Private by default and is shown only if you change it. Your photo and display name are always visible to people who can see your card.

Device & Technical

  • Device UUID — generated locally and stored securely on your device (iOS Keychain / Android encrypted storage); used to support bump matching and device integrity
  • Push token (Apple Push Notification service on iOS, Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android) — to deliver notifications
  • App version, OS version — for debugging and crash reporting

Motion Data (during bump only)

  • Accelerometer and gyroscope readings — captured for approximately 150ms during a double-bump gesture; used only to match your bump with a nearby device
  • Motion data is temporarily stored for bump matching and automatically deleted shortly after processing; it is never used for profiling or sold to third parties

Location (optional)

  • Recording where you met is off by default. We store a location only after you agree to record meeting places and grant location permission, and only after a successful bump
  • Bumping and exchanging cards work without location access; declining has no effect on other features
  • Location information associated with bump events — only when you grant permission and only after a successful bump
  • Location is used to display your social map; it is never used for real-time tracking
  • You can disable location recording at any time in Settings

Phone Contacts (optional)

  • Save to Contacts opens your phone's standard "new contact" screen pre-filled with a connection's card. This does not require contacts permission, and nothing is saved unless you confirm it
  • Contact updates — if you turn this on, DoubleBump asks for contacts permission and only reads and updates the contacts you saved from DoubleBump, comparing them on your device with your connections' latest cards. Changes are applied only with your approval (or automatically if you choose), and fields you edited yourself are never overwritten
  • The link between a saved contact and a DoubleBump user is stored encrypted on your device only
  • We never upload your address book to our servers, and we do not use your contacts to find or suggest other users

Radar (optional)

  • Approximate real-time location — only while you turn Radar on and the App is in the foreground; used to show you that other DoubleBump users are nearby
  • Your location is offset by roughly 60–140 meters before it is shown to anyone — your exact coordinates are never revealed to other users, and Radar never runs in the background
  • When Radar detects a nearby user who is out of Bluetooth range, your nickname and profile photo may be shown to that user (and theirs to you), even if you have never bumped, matched, or connected with them
  • Radar is off by default. You can turn it on or off at any time in Settings → Radar

Bump Memories (optional)

  • Text comments up to 100 characters — written by you about a specific bump
  • Photos — one photo per person per bump, compressed and uploaded by you; visible only to the other person involved, users you're connected with, or users who have temporarily unlocked profiles via a rewarded ad

Chat Messages

  • Text messages you send and receive — you can message people you have connected with by bumping in person. Earlier conversations with anyone else stay readable, but new messages can't be sent to them
  • Search terms you enter to find other users by nickname, to start a conversation
  • Block and report actions — if you report another user, the reason you select and any details you add are shared with us for review

Usage Data

  • Bump events, relationship levels, and feed activity — stored to operate your social graph
  • Chat and Radar activity (e.g., messages sent, Radar enabled/disabled) — stored to operate messaging and nearby-discovery features
  • Ad interaction events — collected by Google AdMob when you view a rewarded ad

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the bump matching and social graph features
  • To exchange your profile card with people you bump, and keep the card they see up to date when you edit it
  • To show your public web card and its contact file (vCard) when you turn the web card on
  • To display your profile and social links to users you have bumped
  • To let you discover nearby DoubleBump users through Radar
  • To enable 1:1 messaging between people who have connected by bumping
  • To review reports and enforce blocks between users
  • To send push notifications about new connections and messages
  • To show your bump map and bump memory history
  • To serve voluntary rewarded video ads that unlock profile features
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data.

Visible to Other Users

Some data is shared directly with other DoubleBump users as part of how the App works — not with an outside company:

  • Your nickname, profile photo, and social links — shown to users you've bumped
  • Your profile card — each field is shown according to the visibility you chose (Public / Connections / Private)
  • Your web card — off by default. If you turn it on, the Public fields of your card can be viewed by anyone who has your card link or QR code, without a DoubleBump account, and saved as a contact file (vCard). Web cards are marked noindex so search engines do not list them, and they disappear as soon as you turn the web card off or delete your account
  • Friends of your connections (2nd degree) — may see a preview of your card (photo, name, headline), and your Public fields after viewing a rewarded ad. You can turn this off in your card settings. This never shares Connections-only or Private fields
  • Your approximate (offset) location and identity — shown to nearby users if you turn on Radar, even before you've bumped
  • Chat messages you send — visible to the recipient

4. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data — retained until you delete your account
  • Profile card data (including phone number and contact email) — only the latest version is kept, not a history of past values; deleted with your account. Your web card stops working immediately when you turn it off or delete your account
  • Bump event records — temporarily retained for matching, then deleted or marked as expired
  • Relationship and feed data — retained until you delete your account
  • Location data — retained until you delete your account or disable location sharing
  • Bump memory photos and comments — retained until you or your account is deleted
  • Chat messages — retained until you delete your account
  • Radar location — retained only while Radar is on; a stale presence is dimmed after 10 minutes and fully deleted within 24 hours of your last update, or immediately when you turn Radar off
  • Reports — reports you have filed are deleted when you delete your account; reports filed against you are retained for safety and moderation purposes even after account deletion

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & Correction: View and edit your profile, nickname, social links, and memories in the App at any time
  • Profile Card: Choose Public / Connections / Private for each contact field, turn your web card on or off, and turn 2nd-degree discovery on or off at any time
  • Contacts: Turn contact updates off in the App, or revoke contacts permission in your phone's settings. Contacts already saved on your phone stay there and are yours to keep or delete
  • Location: Off by default — turn meeting-place recording on or off in Settings, or revoke permission in your phone's settings
  • Radar: Off by default — turn it on or off anytime in Settings → Radar
  • Chat: Block or report another user from any conversation; a blocked user cannot message you and disappears from your Radar and nearby lists
  • Push Notifications: Disable in Settings → Push notifications, or in your phone's settings
  • Account Deletion: Go to Settings → Delete Account to permanently delete all your data. This action is irreversible.

6. Bluetooth

DoubleBump uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to detect nearby phones during a bump. BLE scanning is used only for proximity detection; we do not store Bluetooth identifiers or pair with other devices. BLE is not used when you are not actively bumping.

7. Children's Privacy

DoubleBump is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, contact us at bangcoderpro@gmail.com.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), Row-Level Security policies on our database, and access tokens with automatic rotation.
